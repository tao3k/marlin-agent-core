#+title: Marlin / ui-hydra - Hydra command groups
#+language: en

* Responsibility

Defines declarative command groups, their keys and their presentation. M-x
lists each group. A group may also declare a Normal-mode entry key in its
=defui-hydra= form. For example, =SPC b g= opens the Buffers group directly.

* Interface and contracts

=interface.ss= declares the Module, and =config.ss= publishes its checked
catalog. =syntax.ss= defines =defui-hydra= and lowers declarations to calls in
=funcs.ss=. The functions validate heads and project the hint, keymap and
commands carried by the prototype in =objects.ss=.
Consumers access the selected =UiHydraScope= through
=marlin-agent-core.init.exports=.

The group hint opens in the minibuffer below the modeline. A group with
=hint: #f= supplies its own layout in the docstring. Otherwise, labels on
its heads fill a table. For example, =(q #f "Quit" column: "Actions")=
starts the Actions column. Subsequent heads inherit that column until they
declare another. Column headings, keys, and labels take their colors from
the selected theme. Columns tile the available width, with smaller margins
in narrow terminals. A blue head exits after running. A red head leaves
the group open.

contributes to the package's =std-make-spec=. See the
